# 1976-91: research for the replay's extension (31 Dec 1975 → 31 Dec 1991)

ROADMAP R80a (ADR-1031). As `1963-75.md`, with one change: **each date in the Events table was read from two pages**,
the English Wikipedia article named (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<title>`) and one page that is not Wikipedia
(the column "Second source"). Both were fetched on 2026-11-14 and the date was taken from the page's own text, not
from memory (five agents, each given the events' names and not a date, read them; the dates were compared here).
Where the two differ, both are given and the row is marked **(d)**, with the date the track should use
**in bold**. A date only one page gave is not in the table: it is in "Unverified" at the end.

**†** after a date: one of the two pages gives the day, the other the same event in its month (or, for Wikipedia,
with no day); the day is the one page's. Every other row has the day from both.

Second sources, short names: **state.gov** history.state.gov (Office of the Historian; "recognised" dates are the
United States' recognition, used where it is the independence day); **UPI** upi.com/Archives (wire stories
1986-91); **LoC** countrystudies.us (Library of Congress country studies); **UN** peacekeeping.un.org; **PA-X**
peaceagreements.org; **UCDP** ucdp.uu.se; **CSM** csmonitor.com; **Brit** britannica.com (a search excerpt, the page
refused fetches); others named by site. Weak second sources (an enthusiast or wire-summary site) are marked *weak*.

Codes are the map's `adm0` (Natural Earth `adm0_a3`, as `1963-75.md`); provinces the `adm1` names as
`public/data/earth/admin1-meta.json.wsz` spells them. Kinds as `1963-75.md`: IND, TER, WAR, INS, INT, ALL, REG, FED.
