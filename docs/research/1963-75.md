# 1963-75: research for the replay's extension (31 Dec 1962 → 31 Dec 1975)

Sources are English Wikipedia articles (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<title>`) unless a URL is given. Dates are the
usual ones; disputed dates are marked (d). Codes are the map's `adm0`, i.e. Natural Earth `adm0_a3`, checked against
`public/data/earth/admin1-meta.json.wsz` (gzip). That is mostly ISO3, but these differ: **PSX** for the West Bank
and Gaza (provinces "West Bank" and "Gaza"; not PSE), **SAH** for Western Sahara (one province, NE's area east of the
berm, while NE's **MAR** already has "Laâyoune-Boujdour-Sakia El Hamra" and "Oued Ed-Dahab"), **CYN** for Northern
Cyprus (one province), **SDS** for South Sudan, **SOL** for Somaliland, **KAS** for Siachen. Kashmir is split across
IND (Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh) and PAK.

Every small state below has adm1 rows (NRU 14, TON 5, FJI 5, MDV 21, MLT 68, BRB 11, GRD 7, BHR 5, MUS 16, COM 3,
STP 2, CPV 22, BHS 30, SYC 26, SGP 5). Whether each covers even one cell at the map's resolution is **unverified**
(`areaKm2` is 0 in the metadata). Nauru, Tonga, Malé and Grenada are probably sub-cell; Fiji, Bahamas, Cape Verde,
Comoros and Mauritius probably have a few cells.

Kinds: IND, TER, WAR, INS, INT, ALL, REG (fascism / authoritarian / democracy / communism / monarchy), FED.

## Checkpoints (maps)

1. **1964-12-31**: Malaysia with Sabah, Sarawak, Singapore; Kenya, Tanzania (Zanzibar merged), Malawi, Zambia, Malta
   independent; Papua Indonesian; the US air war over Vietnam, Viet Cong in the Delta; the Simba "People's Republic"
   in the eastern Congo just broken (Stanleyville 11-24); the Aden Emergency; Brazil military.
2. **1967-06-11**: Israel holds Sinai, Gaza, the West Bank and the Golan. Biafra holds Nigeria's Eastern Region.
   Singapore is separate; Rhodesia is under the UDI; Guyana, Botswana, Lesotho and Barbados are independent.
   Greece has its junta, Indonesia has Suharto, Ghana and Nigeria are military, and US ground troops are in Vietnam.
3. **1971-12-17**: Bangladesh independent; India in Sindh/Punjab pockets to 1972. Bahrain, Qatar and the UAE are
   independent; South Yemen is a communist state, Libya is Gaddafi's and Cambodia is Lon Nol's, with the Khmer Rouge and NVA in the
   east. The Pathet Lao hold eastern Laos; Biafra is gone.
4. **1973-10-25**: after the Yom Kippur War: Egypt's strip east of the canal, Israel's pocket west of it, the Syrian
   bulge; the US out of Vietnam (PRG zones); Guinea-Bissau's declared state; Chile under Pinochet, Afghanistan a
   republic, the Bahamas independent; Okinawa Japanese; the Aouzou strip Libyan.
5. **1974-08-16**: Cyprus partitioned (CYN Turkish-held); Greece a democracy; Portugal's revolution, its African
   wars winding down; the Sinai and Golan disengagement lines (Quneitra back to Syria).
6. **1975-12-31**:
   - Southeast Asia: Vietnam is under communist rule, with South Vietnam still formally the PRG's state; Cambodia is
     Democratic Kampuchea; Laos is the LPDR; Indonesia holds East Timor's coast against Fretilin.
   - Africa: Mozambique, Cape Verde, São Tomé and the Comoros are independent. Angola is split three ways, with
     Cuban and South African troops in. Ethiopia is under the Derg, with Eritrean rebels in the countryside.
     Western Sahara is split between Morocco (Laayoune, Smara), Mauritania (La Güera) and the Polisario (the east),
     with Spain not yet fully gone.
   - Elsewhere: PNG and Suriname are independent; Sikkim is Indian; Spain has a king after Franco; Dhofar is
     pacified.
